### 2026-09-02 · arXiv:2609.00621
- **Source:** Zhang, Murtaza, Bhatti, Soni, Nie, Wen & Deng (2026), *Control-Data Flow Separation: Stable Prompt Optimization in Multi-Agent LLMs*, arXiv:2609.00621.
- **Result:** Represents execution-critical protocol (message routing, output format, termination) as typed, validated program objects outside the optimizable prompt text, and reports 100% eventual protocol validity with improved task performance across three workflows.
- **Bears on:** Related Work Map row "Referee boundary in self-revision loops" — supports.
- **Why:** It write-protects one constituent (the execution protocol) from the optimizer while leaving content optimizable, an instance of the separation-of-duties defense the row lists among its anchors; it reports protocol validity rather than a held-out-versus-observed gap, so it bears on the row's defense side, not on the measured ceiling result.
- **Recorded by:** example (drafted for review, 2026-09-02); the maintainer decides whether it stays

### 2026-09-03 · DOI:10.3390/e28050523
- **Source:** Ikegami, Kojima & Kashiwagi (2026), *Community First Theory: How Collective Organization Generates Individual Diversity*, Entropy 28(5):523, DOI:10.3390/e28050523.
- **Result:** Introduces non-trivial information closure (NTIC) to distinguish self-determined from community-distributed temporal predictability and reports a transient information-closed regime in tracked Tetrahymena populations, with greater divergence from parental phenotypes in that regime.
- **Bears on:** Related Work Map row "Fractal Architecture of Emergence" — supports.
- **Why:** It supplies a concrete information-theoretic quantity for locating a transition between collective coupling and situated individuality in one biological system; it does not establish fractality or a universal cross-scale law.
- **Recorded by:** maintainer
