# Mechanistic Discovery Needs Methodological Separation

**Status:** Exploratory note — not a repository claim.

**Trigger.** Wang et al. (2026), [*Mechanist: AI as a Scientific Instrument for Discovering the Mechanisms of Intelligence*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12036), arXiv:2608.12036.

An automated discovery loop can perform several scientific roles without making them epistemically independent. If one shared trajectory generates a hypothesis, selects an intervention, interprets its measurement, and confirms success, the loop can stabilize its preferred explanation while appearing methodologically diverse.

The architectural question is therefore not how many agents or methods participate, but which information, write, and veto rights remain separated. A useful comparison would hold the task fixed across a shared loop, separated roles with shared memory, and a validator blinded to the generating narrative. A mechanistic claim becomes stronger only when alternatives and discriminating interventions are not controlled by the explanation being tested.

This is a methodological review question, not a proposal to build another multi-agent system.
