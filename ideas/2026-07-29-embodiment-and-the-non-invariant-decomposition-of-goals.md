# Embodiment and the Non-Invariant Decomposition of Goals

**Status:** Exploratory note, since promoted to a theory essay. The worked-out
version — three readings of the claim, what Bennett establishes, the effective
goal space quotient, and the alignment conjecture — lives at
[Embodiment and the Non-Invariant Decomposition of Goals](../theory/optimization/embodiment-and-the-non-invariant-decomposition-of-goals.md).

**Encounter.** Bennett's AGI-26 paper argues that realised success is a relation
among controller, embodiment, environment, task, and evaluator — not an
intrinsic software quantity. Read against Bostrom's orthogonality thesis, this
seemed to threaten the intelligence/goal decomposition itself. The disturbance
that started the note: maybe intelligence and goals are separable *as concepts*
while no implementation-invariant factorisation of realised success exists.

**Open question preserved here.** Which transport makes a goal identical across
bodies rather than merely similarly named?
