Collective Agency — intervention budget review, 2026-09-08

Scope: a closed-form geometric check of option 6A. No oscillator trajectories,
estimators, benchmark implementation, or tests of H1–H5 were run. Existing exact
parity and information-distribution controls are already complete and unchanged.

Finding
The candidate calls for matching sum |delta theta_i| but does not freeze whether
angles are endpoint distances on the circle or unwrapped travel. For shortest
endpoint distances, each oscillator can move by at most pi. Consider 16 phases,
eight at +pi/4 and eight at -pi/4. Their mean phase is zero and r=sqrt(2)/2,
so this is not an undefined-mean corner case. At lambda=0.5, every component moves
pi/8 and the macro intervention costs 2*pi. A single oscillator cannot match that
budget. Contracting any half with the same lambda costs pi, only half the macro
budget. The latter arm needs an explicit rescaling rule too.

Counting an unwrapped 2*pi kick instead makes a full turn cost nonzero while
leaving the endpoint unchanged. Such a path or actuator-effort budget is possible,
but it is a different intervention definition and must be explicitly justified.

Concrete amendment for independent review, NOT adopted in the candidate
Use shortest endpoint angular distance and a shared total budget capped below
pi (for example pi/2). Scale each arm to that budget, including the macro arm;
this replaces the fixed lambda=0.5 by a state-dependent strength. A random-half
arm whose contraction support cannot spend that budget needs a preregistered
eligibility or alternate-direction rule. The distribution of excluded states
would have to be reported. Alternatively, drop the one-oscillator control and
justify another matched allocation. These alternatives must not be selected
after observing intervention outcomes.

Exact blocker
A reviewed freeze must choose the angle/budget convention and the matching and
ineligible-state rules, in addition to resolving the six already recorded PI
questions, outcome horizon and recovery epsilon. This check resolves none of
those decisions by fiat. freeze-candidate.json and both false authorization flags
are preserved byte-for-byte. No benchmark implementation has been started.

Completed half-support calculation, 2026-09-10
For a selected half H, let D_H = sum_H |wrap(theta_i-psi)|. If a contraction
does not overshoot the mean (strength between zero and one), it can spend
exactly the interval [0,D_H]. Allowing separate strengths for the macro and
half arms, the four arms have common budgets exactly [0,min(D_H,pi)]: the
whole-group capacity contains D_H, a random-direction arm can copy the macro
magnitudes, and a single oscillator can move at most pi. For any admissible
positive B, strengths B/D_all and B/D_H construct matching contractions.
For B=0, use zero strengths. This is a feasibility proof, not a chosen rule.

The checker now enumerates all C(16,8)=12,870 halves for two fixed states.
In the original fixture every half can match a pi/2-capped macro budget after
rescaling, although none can match the original 2*pi across all four arms.
Now consider eight phases at zero, four at +pi/4 and four at -pi/4. Its mean
phase is zero, coherence is (1+sqrt(2)/2)/2, and lambda=0.5 costs pi. A half
with k off-mean components has capacity k*pi/4, and there are C(8,k)^2 such
halves. Thus 3,985 halves cannot match the requested pi, and 65 still cannot
match a pi/2 cap. The all-zero half has zero capacity at positive coherence.
Consequently no positive shared budget works for every half in this state.

This closes the geometric question behind the proposed amendment: a global
cap alone is insufficient. A future rule must account for the selected half's
capacity, permit zero-budget cases, exclude or redraw supports, change the
half-control definition, or remove that arm. These choices alter the tested
intervention or support distribution and remain external freeze decisions.
The result file adds both exhaustive support counts; tests independently
check the binomial counts, constructive bounds, circular symmetries, invalid
supports, and reproducibility of the saved certificate. No outcomes were used.
