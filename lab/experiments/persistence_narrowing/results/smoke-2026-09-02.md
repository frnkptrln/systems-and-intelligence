# Smoke record — 2026-09-02

**What ran.** A smoke grid of one seed and two evidence rows (2 families × 3 conditions × 2 rows × 256 rules = 3,072 runs), invoked while `persistence_narrowing.py` was being written and before `README.md` was committed. Its purpose was to check that the loop runs, that the `none` condition matches the referee benchmark, and that the held-out == ceiling identity survives the introduction of memory.

**What was seen.** `heldout_equals_ceiling_identity` true in all three conditions of the `full` family. In the `affine` family, mean observed rose under `memory` relative to `none` (noted at the time as 0.765 → 0.854) and mean held-out rose with it (noted as 0.68 → 0.74); mean pull to memory and mean distinct proposals fell under `memory`. The `invalidation` figures were not inspected beyond the identity check. The figures are as noted during the run; the exact subgrid values are the ones in `ci_subgrid.json`.

**What was not seen.** The full grid (2 seeds, 8 rows) and any magnitude on it. No result file was written by the smoke run.

**Reproduction.** `results/ci_subgrid.json`, written by `--save`, is the declared CI subgrid (1 seed, 2 rows) recomputed; `tests/test_persistence_narrowing.py` pins it.
