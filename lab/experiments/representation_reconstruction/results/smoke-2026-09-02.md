# Smoke record — 2026-09-02

**What ran.** One seed over the full rule family (256 rules × 2 initial conditions × 3 noise levels = 1,536 runs per representation, six representations), invoked while `representation_reconstruction.py` was being written and before `README.md` was committed. Its purpose was to check that the encodings and the conjugate rule maps are right and to time a seed (about 64 s).

**What was seen.** Class size equal to the `raw` class size in every one of the 1,536 runs for each invertible representation; the minimal description size of the transformed truth changed for 98 of 256 rules under `complement` and for none under `reflect`; contradictions in most random-initial-condition runs under `block_or2` and `majority3`. The direction of the lossy search cost was not inspected.

**What was not seen.** The second seed and the full-grid means. No result file was written by the smoke run.

**Reproduction.** `results/ci_subgrid.json`, written by `--save`, is the declared CI subgrid (1 seed, every eighth rule, both initial conditions, noise 0 and 0.1) recomputed; `tests/test_representation_reconstruction.py` pins it.
