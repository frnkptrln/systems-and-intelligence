Dear Editors of Artificial Life,

Please consider "The Viable Corridor: A Constraint-Architecture Framework for
Survivable Multi-Agent Optimization" as an Article.

The manuscript examines survivability in synthetic multi-agent systems, fitting
the journal's interest in software models that clarify life-like phenomena. Its
question is how constraint architecture, rather than individual capability alone,
affects long-time viability. A coupled ODE and a stochastic agent ecology explore
resource concentration, coherence and substrate limits. The contribution is a
model-internal demonstration of capability loading multiple constraints and the
limits of single-axis responses. The two models share an author and toolchain;
transfer to real systems remains an uncalibrated hypothesis.
