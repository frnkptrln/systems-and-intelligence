# Paths through the Notebook

**Status:** Reader orientation — suggested walks, not an order of prerequisites.

A question can take you across several kinds of work. These paths offer a few such
crossings. Each stop has a reason to be next; you can also leave the path whenever
something else becomes interesting.

For one continuous introduction, read [Begin with a Sound](synthesis.md).

## Memory and return

*What survives when everything changes?*

Begin with [What a World Can Remember](../theory/narrative/what-a-world-can-remember.md).
An old song opens the distinction between keeping a record and keeping an occasion
possible. The essay moves through a venue, a workshop, and an imagined city.

In [The Weather Between Us](../fiction/20_the_weather_between_us.md), that city acquires
a maintenance department and a voice. The story lets the question become a problem
in someone's life, without resolving what the voice is.

If the missing boundary troubles you, follow
[The Agent Is Not Where the Model Ends](../theory/identity/the-agent-is-not-where-the-model-ends.md).
It asks how tools, bodies, interfaces, and environments enter an account of capability.
The [Situated-Stack Benchmark](../lab/benchmarks/situated-stack/README.md) then gives you
a small, specified arrangement in which those dependencies can be inspected.

To stay with people and time, take another turn into
[From Action to Culture](../theory/emergence/from-action-to-culture.md): a record of a
practice and the ability to enact it make different demands on those who inherit it.

## Rules and worlds

*How does a small change become a different world?*

Start inside the [Web Emergence Explorer](interactive/web-explorer.md). Draw a few
cells and let the field evolve. Changing the rule or the starting state gives you
different ways to disturb what happens next.

[From Trace to Generator](../theory/emergence/trace-to-generator.md) reverses your position.
Now you see the outcome and ask what process could have produced it. Several
processes may fit; the visible pattern need not identify its cause.

The [Inverse-Reconstruction Benchmark](../lab/benchmarks/inverse-reconstruction/README.md)
makes selected versions of that problem small enough to enumerate. Read its
[honest finding](../lab/benchmarks/inverse-reconstruction/README.md#the-honest-finding)
before the successive experiments. Knowing which family a rule belongs to changes
the task substantially.

[The Understudy](../fiction/17_the_understudy.md) moves the ambiguity into a tribunal.
The people there cannot wait for perfect identification before deciding how to act.
The story changes the stakes; it does not supply evidence for the benchmark.

For the mathematical language behind the experiments, turn to the
[Foundations Reconstruction](../theory/core/mathematical-axioms.md).

## Living together

*What lets a shared world continue without making everyone the same?*

Enter through [Kitchen Networks](../fiction/08_kitchen_networks_and_civic_homeostasis.md).
Informal care becomes visible as something a civic system depends on. Stay with
the people in the story before trying to turn their relations into a model.

[Cooperative Intelligence at the Separatrix](../theory/symbiotic/cooperative-intelligence-at-the-separatrix.md)
asks what useful cooperation might require: difference, shared work, revision,
and the ability to refuse. Its larger proposal remains a hypothesis.

The working log [Latency as Mercy](../logs/012_latency-as-mercy.md) focuses on one
choice inside such arrangements: when to wait. Its failure cases matter as much
as the proposal; delay can also prevent care.

[Optimization and Its Blindness](../theory/optimization/optimization-and-its-blindness.md)
widens the question to the conditions an objective leaves out. From there,
[The Viable Corridor](../papers/viable-corridor.md) offers a conditional formal model
with explicit assumptions and a recorded sufficiency correction.

You can also leave the formal path through
[The Last Unmeasured Room](../logs/011_the-last-unmeasured-room-protocol.md), an
exploratory design for places a city does not continually inspect.
