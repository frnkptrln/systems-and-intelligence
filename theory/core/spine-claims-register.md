# Spine Claims Register — Formal Statements and Status

**Status:** Working Note — the one table in which every load-bearing claim of the spine has a formal statement, a five-valued status, a pointer to its proof, counterexample, or experiment, and a falsifier. It is thesis content, not a status page: the two meta registers ([Core Claims](../../meta/repository-meta/core-claims.md), [Identification Claims](../../meta/repository-meta/identification-claims.md)) keep the artifact-by-evidential-kind bookkeeping and are not replaced; this table cross-links to them and adds the formal column and the status vocabulary they do not carry.
**Lane:** Theory — synthesis layer.
**Created:** 2026-09-21
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-21
**Review trigger:** a pinning test named below changing or failing; a new experiment result touching a row; a maintainer decision on any status.
**Provenance:** Drafted 2026-09-21 in an agent session working from the maintainer's Second Founding brief. Every status was assigned from the cited artifact's own wording and every number was read from a frozen README result or a named test; nothing was re-run. Definitions referenced as D1–D12 are in [The Spine as a Discrete Object](spine-formal-definitions.md).

**Status vocabulary (fixed by the brief):**

- **proved** — a theorem, or an exact finite computation that a named test recomputes;
- **supported** — measured in declared testbeds with frozen numbers; not a theorem, and no claim beyond the testbeds;
- **conjectured** — stated, falsifiable in principle, without a decisive instrument;
- **falsified** — refuted by a counterexample or by a preregistered run that went the other way;
- **retracted** — withdrawn by the foundations audit or a later correction, retained as research history.

A status here never changes a home file's own status line; where the two vocabularies differ, the mapping is stated in the pointer.
